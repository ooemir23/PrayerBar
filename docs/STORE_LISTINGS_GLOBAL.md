> This pure native C++20 Win32 desktop utility renders a lightweight prayer-time countdown band anchored adjacent to the Windows taskbar (`Shell_TrayWnd` / `Shell_NotifyIconW`) alongside an on-demand Fluent flyout, monthly calendar (`Imsakiye`), and settings window. `runFullTrust` is required for taskbar window positioning (`SetWindowPos` + `SetWinEventHook`), WinHTTP prayer schedule caching, and optional Windows Startup registration. ## ASO Playbook (store ranking)

1. **Title carries the keywords.** Store search weighs the title most; every locale title is `PrayerBar: <"prayer times" in that language> & Adhan`.
2. **Keywords = what people type, not features.** Cover spelling variants (adhan / azan / athan, salah / salat / namaz). Do not repeat words already in the title.
3. **Screenshots per locale.** Screenshot 1 = zoomed taskbar countdown with a one-line caption; screenshot 2 = flyout; 3 = Imsakiye; 4 = notifications; 5 = settings. Capture each locale's screenshots with the app in that language.
4. **Ramadan window.** Ramadan 2027 starts around 8 Feb 2027. Be live and reviewed by mid-January; from Jan to Mar append "Ramadan" / "Ramazan" / "رمضان" to the short description and put the Imsakiye screenshot first.
5. **Ratings.** The app asks once for a Store rating after 7 days of use (tray notification → Store review page). Reply to every review in Partner Center during the first months.
6. **Category.** Primary: Productivity (less crowded; taskbar utility angle). Secondary: Lifestyle.
7. **Free tier stays generous and ad-free.** Call this out in the first line of every description.
8. **Covered markets:** tr, en, ar, de, fr, id, ur, bn, ms, fa, ru, es. Next candidates: Uzbek (uz), Kazakh (kk), Hindi (hi), Somali (so).
