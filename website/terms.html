Terms of Service & Refund Policy (English)

Last updated: November 2026

1. Free & Pro Licensing

Core prayer times, daily notifications, built-in countdowns, and viewing monthly/Ramadan schedules are permanently free and ad-free. A paid PrayerBar Pro license (Annual or One-Time Lifetime) unlocks additional flyout layouts, extra taskbar styles, custom audio files, custom prayer names, export/print tools, and settings backup for up to 3 Windows devices via your Microsoft Store account.

2. 14-Day Free Pro Trial & Microsoft Store Refunds

Every new installation includes a 14-day free Pro trial with no credit card required. Paid purchases are processed and governed by the Microsoft Store refund terms and your Microsoft Account order history.

3. Prayer Time Accuracy Disclaimer

Offline astronomical calculations and optional Aladhan schedules provide accurate estimates, and manual minute offsets allow alignment with local mosque boards. Users remain responsible for verifying critical fasting times (Suhoor / Iftar) against their local religious authority.
