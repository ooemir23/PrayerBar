Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: November 2026

1. Zero Telemetry & No Personal Tracking

PrayerBar does not collect, track, sell, or transmit your personal data, behavioral analytics, usage telemetry, or continuous location history.

2. Local Storage Only

Your chosen city coordinates, notification preferences, display styles, cached monthly schedules, and license status are stored strictly on your Windows device inside %LocalAppData%\PrayerBar (with an anonymous first-launch day stamp in HKCU\Software\PrayerBar for the 14-day local Pro trial).

3. Optional Network Connections

  • Monthly Prayer Schedule Sync: If enabled in Settings, the app sends an anonymous HTTPS GET request once per month to api.aladhan.com with your configured city coordinates and calculation method. You can disable this anytime for 100% offline astronomical prayer calculation.
  • One-Time Location Auto-Detection: Only when you explicitly press the "Auto-Detect Location" button, a single IP geolocation lookup (ipwho.is / get.geojs.io) is performed to prefill your nearest city and regional calculation method.

4. Payments & Data Processors

All purchases are processed by the Microsoft Store under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; PrayerBar never sees or stores payment details.

PrayerBar; ad, kullanım telemetrisi, reklam kimliği veya arka plan konum geçmişi toplamaz, üçüncü taraflarla paylaşmaz.

2. Yalnızca Yerel Depolama

Tüm ödemeler Microsoft Store tarafından işlenir; ödeme bilgileriniz uygulamaya asla iletilmez.
